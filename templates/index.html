About BloodLink

BloodLink is a DBMS college project built at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala. It demonstrates how relational databases, stored procedures, and triggers can power a real-world life-saving application that connects blood donors with patients in need.

UCS310 — Database Management Systems  |  B.Tech 2nd Year  |  Even Semester 2025–26
🩸

🔍 Find Donors

Search available blood donors instantly by blood group and city using an optimized stored procedure.

⚡ DB Triggers

A BEFORE INSERT trigger automatically marks donors unavailable if they donated within the last 90 days.

🚨 Emergency

Submit emergency requests with urgency levels. Admins can view and act on all requests in real time.

📊 Dashboard

Live statistics showing total donors, available donors, and emergency requests — all from live DB queries.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Node.js + Express backend, MySQL2 with connection pooling, plain HTML/CSS/JS frontend — no frameworks.

🗄️ Schema

Two core tables: donors and requests, with a stored procedure, trigger, and SQL function.

Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about donating blood and using BloodLink
Who can donate blood? ▾
Anyone between 18–65 years of age, weighing at least 50 kg, and in good health can donate blood. You must not have donated blood in the last 90 days.
How often can I donate? ▾
Whole blood can be donated once every 90 days (3 months). This is why our system automatically marks donors as unavailable for 90 days after each donation.
Is my information safe? ▾
Your information is stored securely in our database and is only used to match you with patients in need. We do not share your data with any third parties.
What happens after I register? ▾
Once registered, you will appear in search results when patients in your city search for your blood group. They can contact you directly using the phone number you provided.
How does the emergency request work? ▾
Submitting an emergency request creates a record in our database. Admins and volunteers can view all emergency requests and manually contact matching available donors.
What blood groups are compatible? ▾
O- is the universal donor (compatible with all groups). AB+ is the universal recipient. Always confirm compatibility with medical staff. Our search tool finds exact matches for safety.
Is this service free? ▾
Yes, BloodLink is completely free for both donors and patients. It is a college project developed to demonstrate real-world database applications.
